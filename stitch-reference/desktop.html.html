Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Commercial Registration (CR) in Oman? expand_more
A Commercial Registration (CR) is the foundational legal document required to conduct any business activity in the Sultanate of Oman. Issued by the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MOCIIP), it serves as your official business identity.
Can a foreign investor own 100% of a company in Oman? expand_more
Yes, under the Foreign Capital Investment Law (FCIL) enacted in 2020, foreign investors can own 100% of a Limited Liability Company (LLC) in Oman for most commercial activities, without the need for a local Omani partner.
What are the costs associated with registering a company? expand_more
Costs vary depending on the legal structure, required share capital, and specific licensing required for your activities. OSBIC provides a transparent, all-inclusive pricing model during your initial consultation so you know exactly what to expect before starting.
How long does the company registration process take? expand_more
With all documents prepared correctly, obtaining the initial CR can take as little as 3-5 working days. However, obtaining specific operational licenses from relevant ministries may take additional time depending on the business activity.
Does OSBIC provide support with operational licenses? expand_more
Absolutely. Beyond the initial CR issuance, we offer end-to-end support for acquiring municipal licenses, specific ministry approvals (e.g., Ministry of Health, ROP), visa processing, and tax registration to ensure you are fully operational and compliant.
